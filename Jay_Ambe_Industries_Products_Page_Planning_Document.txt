Approved scope: 4 product categories, 33 products and 1 custom fabrication division


Products Page Strategy
The Products page must not feel like a basic online catalogue. It should present Jay AMBE Industries as a manufacturer of complete stainless-steel solutions for dairy, food processing, institutional kitchens and hygienic process applications.
The page should help a buyer move from broad solution discovery to a technically informative product page and finally to a quotation or project discussion.
Buyer journey
Understand product divisions -> choose a solution category -> review relevant equipment -> open technical product page -> compare options -> request a quote or discuss a custom requirement

1. Products Page Hero
SECTION GOAL
Position the company as a stainless-steel equipment manufacturer and explain the main industries and applications covered by the product range.
RECOMMENDED LAYOUT
Use a medium-height, image-led hero with a strong heading, one concise paragraph, category breadcrumb and two clear actions. The visual should use a real group of AMBE products or a real factory photograph.
CONTENT TO INCLUDE
* Main heading focused on stainless-steel dairy, process and institutional equipment.
* Short description covering standard and custom manufacturing.
* Mention food-grade SS304 and SS316 capability without overloading the hero.
* Keep technical detail for the product sections below.
PRIMARY ACTIONS
* Explore Product Categories
* Request Product Catalogue
IMAGE / VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
* Real AMBE equipment group photograph or manufacturing image.
* No fake composite of unrelated machinery.
AVOID
* Generic marketing headlines.
* Prices or discount messaging.
* AI-generated factory imagery.
* Full-screen hero that pushes products too far down.

2. Product Category Navigation
SECTION GOAL
Let buyers quickly move to the product family relevant to their operation.
RECOMMENDED LAYOUT
Use a clean sticky or in-page category navigation with five labels. On desktop it can be horizontal; on mobile it should become a compact select menu or scrollable tab row with clear active state.
CONTENT TO INCLUDE
* Milk Collection & Handling.
* Dairy Processing Equipment.
* Institutional & Mega Kitchen Equipment.
* Process & Storage Equipment.
* Custom Stainless-Steel Fabrication.
PRIMARY ACTIONS
* Jump to Category
IMAGE / VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
* Simple text-first navigation with optional small category icon.
* Maintain the current global typography and colour system.
AVOID
* A second full website navigation.
* Large decorative cards before the actual products.
* Filters that hide products without clear reset behaviour.

Reusable Product Detail Page Template
All 33 approved products should use one reusable React template. Product-specific content must come from structured data so that updates remain consistent and easy to verify.
1. Product Hero
Product name, category, concise technical summary, main image, Request a Quote and Download Brochure.
2. Product Image Gallery
Front view, side view, construction detail, internal view where relevant, installed application and packaging/dispatch.
3. Product Overview
What the product is, its purpose, main users and main construction approach.
4. Key Features
Product-specific benefits and construction features. Temporary generic features must be clearly marked pending verification.
5. Applications
Only relevant sectors and uses for that product.
6. Technical Specifications
Structured table for capacity, dimensions, material, thickness, finish, motor, electrical, heating/cooling, outlet and customisation.
7. Capacity Options
Verified standard capacities when supplied; otherwise use safe temporary wording.
8. Material & Construction
SS grade, contact-surface material, finish, insulation, jacket or frame details where applicable.
9. Customisation Options
Capacity, dimensions, grade, heating, cooling, agitation, tilting, lid, outlet, branding and packaging.
10. Quality & Testing
Only verified material, dimensional, weld, leak, functional, finish and final inspection claims.
11. Accessories
Standard and optional accessories, clearly separated.
12. Packaging & Delivery
Domestic/export packaging, dispatch method and temporary delivery wording.
13. Related Products
Three or four relevant products from the same or connected category.
14. Final CTA
Request a Quote, Discuss Your Requirement, Upload Your Drawing and Contact Export Team.

The user has approved temporary dummy information for missing technical fields. Dummy values must never look like confirmed engineering data. Use transparent placeholder wording rather than invented numerical specifications.

Keep all category, product and temporary verification data in structured JSON or JavaScript objects. Do not hard-code product content directly inside page components.
* categories: id, slug, name, shortDescription, heroImage, order.
* products: id, slug, categoryId, name, shortDescription, overview, images, applications, features, specifications, capacities, materials, customisation, accessories, packaging, brochure, relatedProducts.
* verificationStatus: verified, partially_verified or pending.
* isActive: temporary client-confirmation field.
* isStandardProduct: pending confirmation where unknown.
* isExportSuitable: pending confirmation where unknown.
* oemAvailable: pending confirmation where unknown.
* placeholderNotes: internal-only notes that must never appear in the final public interface.
Important development rule
Every unverified specification must carry verificationStatus: "pending" or an equivalent flag.
The public interface should display safe temporary wording, while the internal data file keeps a clear list of fields awaiting client approval.
Do not publish fake numeric values merely to make a specification table look complete.

AI Editor Implementation Rules
* Use only the approved product categories and products in this document.
* Do not invent additional products or technical specifications.
* Do not show prices on the Products landing page or product-detail pages.
* Use real AMBE photography when supplied; clearly labelled placeholders may be used temporarily.
* Do not make the page look like a marketplace or ecommerce store.
* Do not use repeated AI-style rounded cards for every section.
* Keep technical information readable and consistent across all product pages.
* Make every Request a Quote action open or scroll to a working enquiry form with the product preselected.
* Preserve the current global header, footer, typography and minimal colour system.
* Do not expose internal placeholder notes or verification flags on the public page.
Final Products Page Flow
1. Understand the complete AMBE product range.
2. Choose the relevant solution category.
3. Review products without marketplace-style clutter.
4. Open a technically structured product-detail page.
5. Review applications, specifications, capacities and customisation.
6. Download approved technical material or request it.

Final design objective
The Products page should show breadth without feeling like a catalogue dump.

Every product path should naturally lead toward a technical discussion or quotation.
